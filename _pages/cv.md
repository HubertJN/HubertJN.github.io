## Profile
I am a final-year PhD student in the [Centre for Doctoral Training in Modelling of Heterogeneous Systems](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/hetsys/) at the University of Warwick, based in the Department of Physics under the supervision of Prof. David Quigley. My training included a one-year CDT programme covering materials simulation and predictive modelling techniques for a Postgraduate Diploma, and I specialise in enhanced Monte Carlo sampling and predictive machine learning for complex physical systems. My research has focused on high-entropy alloys, phase transitions, thermodynamic properties, and machine-learned models for collective variables and nucleation rates.

## Research experience
* **September 2022 - Present**: PhD Researcher, Department of Physics, University of Warwick, UK 
  * Supervisor: [Prof. David Quigley](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/physics/staff/academic/quigley/) (University of Warwick)  
  * Project Title: "<i>Memory matters: Beyond Markovian models of rare-event kinetics</i>"  
  * Summary: My research centres on rare-event kinetics and enhanced Monte Carlo sampling, with applications to phase transitions and thermodynamics in high-entropy alloys. I developed supervised CNN committor predictors as a practical alternative to expensive committor calculations for nucleation-rate estimation, and created a scalable variant of Wang-Landau sampling for calculating densities of states in alloy systems. This work includes analysing parallelisation strategies, improving load balancing across energy windows, and applying flat-histogram sampling to high-entropy alloys.

* **October 2021 - July 2022**: Final-Year Project Student, University of Warwick, UK
  * Supervisor: [Dr. John Marshall](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/physics/staff/academic/marshall/) (University of Warwick)  
  * Project Title: "<i>Classification of neutrino-induced particles, and characterisation of showers at the Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment (DUNE)</i>"  
  * **Summary**: Analysed simulated DUNE detector events in Python to classify neutrino-induced particle signatures and characterise shower topology. Investigated class separability using receiver operating characteristic curves.

* **Summer 2021**: Research Intern, Department of Mathematics, University of Warwick, UK  
  * Supervisor: [Dr. Radu Cimpeanu](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/maths/people/staff/cimpeanu/) (University of Warwick)  
  * Project Title: "<i>Deformation of Droplets at High Speeds</i>"  
  * Summary: Modelled high-speed droplet deformation using Navier-Stokes solvers in [Basilisk](http://basilisk.fr/). Collaborated with a computational fluid dynamics group to review numerical methods and the broader modelling approach.

* **Summer 2020**: Research Intern, School of Engineering, University of Warwick, UK  
  * Supervisor: [Dr. Peter Brommer](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/eng/people/staff/brommer/) (University of Warwick)  
  * Project Title: "<i>Monte Carlo Study of the Equilibrium State of NiAl Crystal</i>"  
  * Summary: Developed a Markov chain Monte Carlo sampler in Python with [LAMMPS](https://docs.lammps.org/) as the atomistic simulation engine. Analysed simulation trajectories to probe equilibrium behaviour and identify transition bands in the sampled state space.

* **Summer 2019**: Research Intern, Department of Physics, University of Warwick, UK
  * Supervisor: [Dr. John Duffy](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/physics/staff/academic/duffy/) (University of Warwick)
  * Project Title: "<i>Phase Transitions and Dielectric Properties of Nano-confined Water</i>"
  * Summary: Operated a liquid-nitrogen-cooled cryostat and vacuum system to run LabVIEW automated temperature-controlled dielectric measurements on a nanoporous medium saturated with water.
